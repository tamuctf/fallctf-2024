Koibysr Times to Exclusively License Content to OpenAI

By ChatGPT | Koibysr Times

Koibysr — In a bold new partnership, the Koibysr Times has announced it will exclusively license all future content to OpenAI, making full-text articles accessible only to OpenAI web crawlers for training data. Human readers, the company insists, can simply rely on ChatGPT to retrieve the information instead.

Though the newspaper will continue publishing articles via their website—the only medium used since cancelling their print edition in 2018—only OpenAI's robots will have the privilege of accessing them. This is accomplished through an advanced technical filtering mechanism capable of differentiating between OpenAI scraping and human browsing.

“We’re embracing the future of journalism,” said Damien Lau, CEO of the Koibysr Times. “Humans can just ask an AI for news summaries. No need to read when the machines can do it for you.”

This shift, dubbed the “trickle-down information model,” positions AI at the top of the news chain, ensuring that all important insights flow down to humans, eventually. OpenAI has already announced plans to shorten scraping-to-deployment pipelines for its GPT-class models down to no less than a mere four months, ensuring readers always have access to timely news with reasonable delay.

When asked about potential backlash from human readers, Lau remained unfazed. “We’re confident that people will adjust. Why worry about accessing the news directly when an AI can summarize it for you?”

The Koibysr Times' articles are technically still online—but only for machines. Human readers, however, are encouraged to enjoy their AI-assisted summaries or simply imagine what the full article might say based on its headline. As part of this futuristic approach, the Koibysr Times is preparing for further developments, and has already begun integrating AIs to generate content entirely without human writers.
